Make a tab from a recording

Pulls the guitar out of a song and writes a draft Guitar Pro tab, using free computers on your private GitHub. Takes about 5 to 10 minutes per song. The draft gets you most of the way; fix notes as you learn it.

To record: play the song out loud from Spotify or YouTube in a quiet room, phone close to the speaker, and record from start to finish. An audio file you own works even better.

Following the recording
Move the notes
Play the song in Spotify and the notes follow it. To line it up, tap “Tap when the guitar starts” the moment the guitar comes in. If it drifts later in the song, double-tap the highway right when you hear the note that's crossing the line, or use the sooner and later buttons at that spot. Each fix only changes that part of the song, so a band that speeds up or slows down still lines up. Auto-align listens for 10 seconds (play it out loud). Keep in sync keeps listening through the mic and corrects small drift by itself.
TV delay 200 ms
Smart View shows the TV a moment late. Fretwork runs ahead by this much so the TV is on time. If notes reach the strings on the TV after you hear them, raise it. If they arrive early, lower it. Turn Guide sound off or keep the TV quiet so the phone's mic only hears your guitar.

Your own teacher, freeFretwork uses your personal Gemini key from Google. Everything runs on your free allowance, and your key stays on this phone.

Link my Gemini

  1. In Chrome, open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Tap Create API key, then copy it. New keys start with AQ. (older ones start with AIza). Either works.
  3. Paste it below and tap Link.

What should it do?

Turn on what you want. Each one picks the right Gemini model by itself. Light ones barely touch your free allowance.

Voice

Pick a voice. Tap play to hear a sample. Nothing is downloaded until you turn the voice on.
How much should it talk?
It never talks while you're playing. It waits for a pause, stops the moment you press play, and won't repeat itself.
Privacy: on Google's free tier, Google may use what's sent to improve its products. Fretwork only sends short practice summaries and your questions. Photos and audio are sent only when you tap a button to send them.
